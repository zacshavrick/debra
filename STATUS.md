# Debra: status

## What the game is
A 3D action RPG where you play a crustacean on the seafloor. Every kill drops a body part, and wearing it changes your stats, attacks and look: you are what you kill. Godot 4.7.2; ships as a Windows exe and a mobile web app.

## Current state
Milestones 1–3 are done: 5 starter crabs, a branching map, an endless generated dive with chimera bosses, 36 creatures, 114 parts, loot tiers up to Gold. Latest build: `builds/Debra-v0.10-windows.zip`. CI builds and publishes through GitHub. Design: `docs/DESIGN.md`; code: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.
